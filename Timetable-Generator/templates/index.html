About The Project

Conflict-Free Timetable Generator Engine developed using Genetic Algorithms.

Project Overview

This academic scheduler is an intelligent, conflict-free timetable generation engine powered by Genetic Algorithms. It optimizes room allocations, faculty schedules, and weekly period constraints to automate institution-wide scheduling. It supports parallel student groups, credit hours, and specialized theory vs. lab assignments, ensuring conflict-free structures with optimal resource usage.

Zero Conflicts

Resolves overlapping teacher, room, and student schedules automatically.

Genetic Evolution

Uses selection, crossover, and mutation mechanisms to search for optimal solutions.

Flexible Constraints

Supports credits, continuous lab sessions, and custom department criteria.
